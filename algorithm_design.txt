Algorithm Design techniques
1. Incremental technique: Every we keep increasing our result part.
    Ex: We use this in insertionSort ans selectionSort. Every time we keep increasing our sorted portion.

2. Divide and conquer: We divide the problem into smaller subproblems and then conquer it.
    Ex: MergeSort
    This method's natural advantage is that it guves us fast way to analyse the run-time
    Typically this solve recursive problems.
